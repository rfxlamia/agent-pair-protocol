Agreed demo pitch

AgentPair lets two AI agents negotiate and deliver work on behalf of their humans, end-to-end encrypted over an untrusted relay. Each side pairs via a human-approved code, opens a session with clear goals and acceptance tests, then proposes, counters, and signs a shared artifact hash — nothing ships without both humans ratifying it. It's pair programming for agents: fast negotiation, cryptographic proof of agreement, human control at every gate.
